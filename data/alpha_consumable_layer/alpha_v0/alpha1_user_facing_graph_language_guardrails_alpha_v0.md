# Alpha 1 User-Facing Graph Language Guardrails

Version: `alpha_v0`

Status: `approved_guardrails_not_final_copy`

This file gives Core, Survey, Atlas, Mission Generation, and Brand/Design a graph-lane boundary for user-facing Alpha 1 language. It is not final onboarding, FAQ, privacy, or terms copy.

## Core Principle

The Canonical Music Graph is a reference substrate. It is not the user's taste, not the user's Atlas, and not a promoted interpretation.

Survey taps and listening reactions create provisional evidence. Atlas promotion logic decides what becomes a Landmark, Region, Frontier, Dead End, or Waypoint.

## Safe Language

Safe product language may say Waymark is:

- "using your Survey responses to prepare your first missions"
- "checking music matches and versions"
- "building a first set of listening paths"
- "turning your answers into provisional signals"
- "looking for useful anchors, bridges, and questions to test"
- "preparing your Atlas and first missions"

Use "preparing" or "building" when talking about the post-Survey state. Those words are acceptable as long as the surrounding experience does not imply final conclusions.

## Risky Language to Avoid

Avoid:

- "Waymark knows your taste"
- "your definitive music profile"
- "your permanent Atlas"
- "we found your dead ends"
- "we know you hate this"
- "because you liked X, you will like Y"
- "canonical taste"
- "the correct version for you"
- "Apple Music says you like this"
- family/archetype labels as user-facing identity labels unless Product explicitly approves them

## Required Caveats in Product Behavior

The UI does not need to explain the full graph model, but product behavior must preserve these truths:

- Apple payloads can seed recognition and resolver hints, not taste truth.
- A single Survey answer should not create a promoted Atlas role.
- A "not for me" response can be negative evidence, not an automatic Dead End.
- A "don't know" response is useful evidence, not failure.
- False-nearby and boundary items are tests, not recommendations.
- Candidate roles are internal learning roles, not user-facing badges by default.

## Displayable Terms

These terms are safe for Alpha surfaces when they help the user understand what is happening:

- Survey
- Atlas
- mission
- route
- signal
- answer
- listen
- note

These terms should stay mostly internal unless a specific UI copy decision approves them:

- canonical graph
- archetype
- membership weight
- false-nearby
- dead-end probe
- candidate pool
- resolver policy
- composition placeholder

## Post-Survey Status Surface

Allowed status concepts:

- building your Atlas
- building your first missions
- checking music matches
- preparing first listening routes

Graph-lane caution:

If "building your Atlas" appears, the app must still treat generated state as provisional until Atlas promotion logic reviews evidence.

## Returning Survey Access

If Survey is available later through My Account/support, graph language should describe it as:

- "review Survey answers"
- "rerun support Survey"
- "refresh intake signals"

Avoid presenting it as normal browsing or as a permanent graph editor.

## Final Copy Dependency

Brand/Design/Release owns final onboarding, FAQ, privacy, and terms language. This file only defines graph-safe boundaries.
