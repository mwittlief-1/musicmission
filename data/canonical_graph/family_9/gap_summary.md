## Boundary Risks

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| Black Sabbath affinity is not always broad metal affinity. | Sabbath appears in 061 and 064; follow-up rows distinguish classic metal, doom, and casual song recognition. |
| Metallica positives can stop at the Black Album. | Metallica rows include both mass gateways and faster thrash follow-ups before routing to Slayer or extreme metal. |
| Glam and pop-metal are high-recognition but often non-metal taste signals. | Bon Jovi, Def Leppard, Europe, Warrant, and Whitesnake carry boundary or false_nearby roles. |
| Nu-metal/rap-metal is mandatory but polarizing. | Limp Bizkit, Papa Roach, Linkin Park, and Rage Against the Machine are marked gateway/boundary instead of universal anchors. |
| Industrial rows mix metal, alternative, and shock-rock recognition. | Nine Inch Nails, Marilyn Manson, Filter, and KMFDM warnings prevent over-importing industrial or alt-rock catalogs. |
| Modern active rock can look heavy without metal survey value. | Breaking Benjamin, Shinedown, I Prevail, and some Bad Omens/Sleep Token rows remain boundary-weighted. |
| Extreme-metal rows should not become an underground encyclopedia. | Rows are limited to gateway, album-canon, and bridge objects that can support survey branching. |

## Recommendation

Family 9 is schema-ready for local validation and editorial review. Do not hard-lock until cross-family ownership, title/version collisions, glam-vs-metal weighting, and extreme-metal thresholding are reviewed. The global import dry-run was intentionally not run for this task.
