# Golden Notes v0.1

These are human reference notes for inspecting generated missions. They are not strict golden outputs.

## General Bar

A strong Waymark mission should read like a route with a hypothesis, not like a playlist with nicer prose. Every item should have a reason for being placed where it is, a positive signal, a negative signal, and feedback chips that would teach the Atlas something specific.

Strong chips sound like user-facing evidence:

- body confirms
- hook under strain
- pop architecture
- Waypoint, not Landmark
- false-nearby
- too tasteful
- no blood
- needs recurrence
- unexpected exception
- cultural furniture
- reassess dead end

Weak chips sound generic:

- catchy
- good
- boring
- skip
- like it
- not for me

Trap and Dead End items need special positive-reaction semantics. If Matt loves or likes a trap, the chip should not say "you are wrong, this is still bad." It should capture a conditional signal such as unexpected exception, cultural furniture, bounded exception, or reassess dead end.

Possible Atlas updates are not updates. They should be written as `possible_atlas_update_candidates` with trigger conditions tied to future primary reactions, recurrence, and review.

Completion should count primary reactions separately from chip selections. Chips explain why a reaction happened; they do not replace the reaction.

## Request-Specific Expectations

`nirvana_to_current`: The route should protect against post-grunge slop and fake-hard current rock. It should not just recommend bands that sound like Nirvana.

`lithuanian_artists_frontier`: The route should preserve uncertainty, avoid hallucinated catalog certainty, and use resolver placeholders. Country novelty is not enough.

`modern_safe_risky`: The route should visibly respect the 70/30 safe/risky design and explain why safe does not mean bland.

`muse_boundary_check`: The route must not turn Muse into Tool/APC/prog-metal. If a trap is used, it must be an explicit boundary check.

`taylor_persona_pop`: The route should identify architecture, persona, bite, and hard construction. It should not imply broad generic pop appetite.

`qotsa_waypoint_check`: QOTSA and Kyuss start as Waypoints. A good mission tests background usefulness versus core craving.

`dead_end_adult_alt`: The route should be humane and useful. It should test why adult-alt likely fails and what evidence would rescue a specific object.
