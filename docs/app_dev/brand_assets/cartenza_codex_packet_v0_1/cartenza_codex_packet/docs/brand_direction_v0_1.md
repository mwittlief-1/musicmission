# Cartenza Brand Direction v0.1

## Brand name

**Cartenza**

Working interpretation: a coined name carrying hints of *carta* / cartography and *cadenza* / musical expression. It should feel like a sophisticated system for mapping personal music taste, not like a generic music player or playlist generator.

## Product promise

Primary:

> **Your music. Mapped.**

Alternates:

- **Your personal music atlas.**
- **Map the music that feels like yours.**
- **Guided listening for the music that is actually yours.**

## Visual mode

**Night Vision**

Desired feel:

- dark
- restrained
- premium
- intelligent
- modern
- slightly sexy
- musical without being literal
- cartographic without looking like a travel app
- mature and stately without being stuffy

Avoid:

- generic waveform logos
- map pins
- loud gradients
- childish exploration/treasure-map motifs
- Spotify-style green or streaming-app cliches
- overly sci-fi orbital UI
- generic AI assistant visuals
- social app or productivity-dashboard aesthetics

## Master logo direction

**Concept 5: Circular Cartographic Monogram / Compass-Seal C**

This is the preferred master direction because it best combines:

- Cartenza as a serious brand system
- C as compass / cartographic monogram
- Atlas and music-taste mapping
- premium Night Vision visual language
- strong app-icon scalability

## Secondary motif direction

Borrow from **Concept 1: Topographic C** for:

- Atlas backgrounds
- onboarding screens
- mission cards
- empty states
- profile / starter-map surfaces
- subtle visual texture behind headings

## Recommended lockups

1. App icon: circular C seal inside dark rounded square.
2. Primary wordmark: `Cartenza` in refined high-contrast serif.
3. Product descriptor: `Music Taste Atlas` or `Your music. Mapped.`
4. Splash screen: app icon above wordmark, short subtitle below.
5. Atlas surface: faint topographic/field lines behind cards and nodes.

## Typography direction

Use a refined editorial/high-contrast serif for the wordmark and headings. Do not ship proprietary fonts unless properly licensed.

Suggested implementation fallbacks:

- iOS / SwiftUI: `.serif` or New York where appropriate.
- Web fallback stack: `ui-serif, "New York", "Iowan Old Style", "Cormorant Garamond", "Playfair Display", Georgia, serif`.
- Body/UI: `Inter`, `SF Pro`, or system sans.

## Color direction

See `tokens/cartenza_night_vision_tokens.json`.

Primary palette:

- Obsidian / near-black background
- Charcoal panels
- soft metallic silver typography
- muted antique gold as signal/waypoint accent
- deep desaturated teal as Atlas/substrate accent

The visual language should support these product concepts:
